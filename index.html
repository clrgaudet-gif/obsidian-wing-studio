The Work

Not manufactured. Not soft.

This is Atlantic Canadian wildlife photographed in real moments. Eagles that own the horizon. Ravens that cut the sky like omens. Foxes that do not pose.

Edits are limited to tone, contrast, crop, and clarity. The bird was not baited, staged, or invented. The dark is already there.

Who Am I?

The memoir. Raw, literary, unfinished in the way truth is unfinished. Coming to self-publish.

Eagles, rivers, cameras, grief, survival, beauty beside the wreckage. The same eyes that hold the branch also hold the pages.

When the book is ready, it will live here too. Until then, the prints are the proof the looking happened.
