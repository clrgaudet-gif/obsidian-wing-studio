About

I capture the raw, untamed moments where light battles shadow in the wild. From the silent power of a raven in the mist to the violent grace of eagles clashing over Moncton’s rivers and forests — my camera doesn’t pretty things up. It tells the truth.

This is wildlife and nature photography with edge — dark, honest, and alive. Nothing soft. Nothing staged. Real birds. Real light. Real wilderness.

Mila Luxx · Obsidian Wing Studio

Selected Work

I don’t shoot pretty sunsets and happy little birds. I hunt the raw, brutal, beautiful moments most people never see — mid-air violence, ravens in the mist, predators doing what they were built to do.
